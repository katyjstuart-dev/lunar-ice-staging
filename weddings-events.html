PLAN THE PRACTICAL BITS

KEEPING THE ICE
READY FOR THE BAR.

Storage at the venue

A freezer is ideal. A chiller, walk-in or cold room can help for shorter holding times. With no cold storage, keep the bags together in the coolest shaded place, out of direct sun and covered with a blanket or foil. Need dedicated storage? We recommend Grizzly Bear Events in Horsham.

How much will you need?

Guest numbers are only the start. The drinks menu, weather, event length and whether you are chilling bottles all affect the quantity. Tell us the plan and we’ll sense-check it with you.

Timing that works

We generally work with a two-hour delivery window, planned around venue access, bar setup and when the ice will actually be needed.

Ice melts. A sensible plan makes all the difference.

THE RIGHT ICE FOR THE JOB

DRINKS. BOTTLE CHILLING. BAR SERVICE.

For weddings and private events, cubed ice usually does the heavy lifting: drinks service plus chilling Champagne, wine, beer and soft drinks. Crushed ice is there when the bar, display or food presentation specifically calls for it.

A typical wedding order is often around 15 bags, but the right amount depends on guest numbers, weather, event length and whether the ice is for drinks, bottle chilling or both. Give us the details and we’ll help you arrive at a sensible quantity.
