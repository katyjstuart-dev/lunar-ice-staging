THE ICE IS ONLY HALF THE JOB

STORAGE, ACCESS
AND TIMING MATTER.

No freezer on site?

Very common. Where possible, a refrigerated/chiller trailer is the best option. If there isn't one, we'll stack the ice in the coolest practical place and out of direct sunlight.

Plans changed?

Weather warmed up? Guest numbers moved? Contact us. We'll do our best to adapt the order around the new plan.

Two-hour delivery slot

It gives us room for traffic, access and the unexpected while keeping the delivery useful for your event schedule.

Unfortunately, we can't negotiate with science.

Ice will melt. Good storage, shade and sensible planning make a huge difference — and we'll talk you through it.
