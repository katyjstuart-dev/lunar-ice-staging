No freezer on site?

Very common. Where possible, a refrigerated/chiller trailer is the best option. We recommend Grizzly Bear Events in Horsham for chiller and freezer trailer hire. If there isn't one, we'll stack the ice in the coolest practical place and out of direct sunlight.

Weather warmed up? Guest numbers moved? Contact us. We'll do our best to adapt the order around the new plan.
