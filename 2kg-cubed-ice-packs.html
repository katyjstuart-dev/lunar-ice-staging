6 × 2KG CUBED ICE

SMALLER BAGS.
EASIER TO HANDLE.

Same idea as cubed ice, just in smaller 2kg bags. Easier to carry, easier behind a bar and much easier to fit into tight freezer space.

THE PRODUCT

6 × 2KG CUBED ICE PACKS

The main reason to choose the 6 × 2kg pack is simple: the individual bags are more manageable.

They are easier for staff to carry around, easier to work with behind a bar and, when freezer space is limited, the smaller bags are much more packable than a full 12kg bag.

You can also split them between different bar stations or freezers without opening a large bag before you need it.

GOOD FOR

Bar work and smaller service points

Limited freezer space

Splitting ice between several stations

Easier lifting and carrying

Home parties and smaller events

Opening only the amount you need

WHEN 12KG IS BETTER

FILLING A BIG TRUG?

Go for the 12kg cubed bags. The ice is free-flowing, the bag is roughly the size of a filled pillowcase and it is much quicker to decant into large trugs, tubs, troughs or bottle-chilling containers.
