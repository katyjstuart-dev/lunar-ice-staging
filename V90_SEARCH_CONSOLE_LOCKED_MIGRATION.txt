TECHNICAL CHECKLIST
- Strong legacy URLs retained where possible.
- One-hop 301s only for genuine moves.
- No redirect chains in declared migration rules.
- Canonicals self-reference retained canonical pages.
- Internal navigation points directly at retained canonical URLs.
- Sitemap contains canonical URLs, not redirect sources.
- robots.txt keeps sitemap discoverable and does not block migrated content.
- HTTPS/www normalisation preserves paths.
- Structured data must remain valid.
- 404 retained.
- Post-launch: resubmit sitemap, inspect priority URLs, monitor Page indexing + redirects + query/page clicks/impressions/CTR/position. Do not request removal of harmless historical URLs just because they are old.

GO-LIVE GATE
Do not deploy until the final migration table and technical validation pass are clean. Do not call the migration successful on launch day; Search Console monitoring after launch is part of the migration.
