FAQS & ADVICE

QUESTIONS?
WE LIKE THOSE.

The useful answers, without the waffle. If your situation is unusual, call us — unusual is often where we are most useful.

How much ice do I need for 100 guests?

For drinking ice only, our usual planning guide is around 10 × 12kg bags per 100 guests. Event length, style and what else the ice is being used for can change the requirement, so we always recommend talking it through.

Can you deliver to a field or a wedding with no useful postcode?

Yes. We regularly deliver to rural venues, fields, marquees and temporary sites. What3Words is extremely useful because it gives us the exact drop location.

Do I need freezer or chilled storage?

Frozen or chilled storage is the best option. If there is no suitable storage, we will stack the ice in the coolest practical location and keep it out of direct sunlight where possible. Ice is still ice, though — unfortunately we cannot negotiate with science.

Can I increase my order if the weather gets hotter?

Contact us as soon as you know. Plans and forecasts change and we will do our best to accommodate an increase, subject to stock, vehicle capacity and route availability.

Do you deliver outside Sussex, Surrey and Kent?

Yes, depending on the requirement. Larger orders can take us into Hampshire, Berkshire, Essex, Buckinghamshire and beyond.

Do you supply mobile bars and caterers?

Absolutely. Mobile bars, high-end caterers, drinks vendors, wedding planners and event teams are a major part of what we do.

Can you deliver to festivals and restricted-access sites?

Yes. We have experience with accreditation, security checkpoints, restricted access and time-sensitive event deliveries.

Cubed or crushed ice?

It depends on what you are using it for. Tell us the job and we will help you choose the most practical product.

How do the delivery slots work?

We generally use two-hour delivery windows. That gives us sensible resilience for traffic, access problems and the unexpected while still giving you a useful arrival window.

Can you do same-day delivery?

Same-day delivery can be possible in select areas when routes and stock allow. Contact us and we will tell you what is realistically available.
