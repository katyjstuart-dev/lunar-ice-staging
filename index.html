ICE PLANNER

A STARTING POINT.
THEN WE SENSE-CHECK IT.

For drinking ice only, our usual planning guide is around 10 × 12kg bags per 100 guests. Chilling is different because the thing you're filling matters — a trough and a rowing boat are not exactly the same job.

Drinking ice

Guest numbers give us the starting point. Event length and style help refine it.

Chilling ice

Tell us what you're filling, the approximate size and how many. We'll advise from experience rather than pretend one calculator fits everything.
