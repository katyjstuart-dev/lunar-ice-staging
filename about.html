OWNER & FOUNDER

HI, I’M KATY.

I founded Lunar Ice with a very clear idea of how I wanted customers to be looked after: properly, personally and without losing the standards you expect from a professional supplier.

My background is in senior product and supplier relationship management, working across supplier partnerships, commercial relationships, product, problem-solving and customer outcomes. I also have B2B and B2C marketing credentials, so I understand both sides of the relationship — what businesses need from a dependable supplier and what private customers need when an event really matters.

I lead Lunar Ice, but the service is delivered as a team. The people working with Lunar Ice operate to the same expectations around communication, reliability, presentation and getting the job done properly.

That is the balance we are aiming for: easy to speak to, flexible and human — with the capability, systems and delivery standards of a much bigger operation.
