THE PERSON BEHIND THE BUSINESS

HI, I’M KATY.

My background is in senior product and supplier relationship management, where strong commercial relationships, clear communication and making things happen were part of the day job.

I worked across supplier partnerships, product, problem-solving and customer outcomes — the kind of role where you have to keep a lot of moving parts aligned and still make the experience feel straightforward for the person at the other end.

I also have B2B and B2C marketing credentials, which means I understand what a business customer expects from a dependable supplier as well as what a private customer needs when they are planning something important.

That experience is exactly how Lunar Ice is run today. We keep things personal, responsive and easy to deal with, while operating with the professionalism, organisation and delivery capability you would expect from a much larger supplier.

So yes, you can speak to a real person. But no, Lunar Ice is not just me, myself and I turning up with a few bags in the boot. We are set up to deliver properly — and to scale the service to the job in front of us.
