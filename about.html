ABOUT LUNAR ICE

OWNER & FOUNDER

HI, I’M KATY.

I set up Lunar Ice to deliver the kind of supplier relationship I had always expected in my own professional life: clear communication, accountability and people who do what they say they are going to do.

Before Lunar Ice, my career was in senior product and supplier relationship management. That background taught me a huge amount about managing suppliers, building strong commercial relationships, solving problems quickly and keeping the customer outcome at the centre of the job. I also have B2B and B2C marketing credentials, which means I understand the expectations on both sides of the relationship.

As Owner & Founder, I lead the standards, direction and customer experience at Lunar Ice. I am supported by a team who work to exactly the same approach: communicate clearly, plan the job properly, turn up prepared and look after the customer from enquiry through to delivery.

That is what we are building Lunar Ice around: a business that feels personal and easy to deal with, backed by the people, vehicles, systems and capability to handle serious requirements when our customers need us to.
