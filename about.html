ABOUT LUNAR ICE

A TEAM BEHIND
EVERY DELIVERY.

Katy Stuart heads up Lunar Ice, backed by a team covering accounts, customer enquiries, marketing, delivery planning, logistics and the deliveries themselves.

AT THE HELM

KATY STUART

Katy leads Lunar Ice and brings more than 20 years of ice-industry experience to the business.

Lunar Ice is not just Katy answering a phone and jumping in a van. There is a team behind it, working across accounts, marketing, customer enquiries, deliveries and logistics.

Katy is at the helm, but everyone is singing from the same hymn sheet: look after the customer, communicate properly and get the ice where it needs to be.

That applies whether we are sorting ice for a garden party, planning a wedding delivery, keeping a festival supplied or moving pallet quantities for a commercial customer.

Customers still get a personal service. They just have a proper team behind it.

Accounts

Planning & logistics

Delivery team

Customer support

Phone, WhatsApp, text and website enquiries, with communication throughout.
