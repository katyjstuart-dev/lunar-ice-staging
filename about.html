ABOUT LUNAR ICE

ICE DELIVERY.
WITHOUT THE MELTDOWN.

Anyone can sell you a bag of ice. We built Lunar Ice around everything that happens before it reaches your hands — the planning, the communication, the timing and the slightly obsessive need to get it right.

Based at Gatwick, we deliver cubed and crushed ice across Sussex, Surrey, Kent and the wider South East. From a garden party to a festival field, a wedding in the middle of nowhere to serious commercial volume, we bring the same standard to every job.

Our navy and green branding and signage make Lunar Ice easy to recognise, but looking professional is only part of it. When we turn up, we want the whole experience to feel considered: the communication beforehand, the condition of the delivery, the way we arrive and the way we deal with people.
