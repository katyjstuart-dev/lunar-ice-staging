A PROPER RUN.

We deliver for weddings, private events, hospitality, bars, caterers and larger events across Kent, with the route planned around the job rather than a generic delivery round.

Country-house weddings, barns, marquees and temporary event sites often need a bit more thought than a normal address. If there’s a venue gate, service entrance, security point or field access, tell us — that’s useful information, not a nuisance.

Sevenoaks, Tunbridge Wells, Maidstone, Ashford, Canterbury, Dartford, Rochester, Folkestone and surrounding areas can all be covered depending on the order and route. For bigger requirements, we can travel further too.

Rural site or awkward entrance?

Send us What3Words and access notes. The exact gate is considerably more useful than “you can't miss it”.
