FESTIVALS & LARGE EVENTS

BIG EVENT?
NO DRAMA.

Need 40 bags before the gates open? Pallets to the main bar? Top-ups through the weekend? That’s the sort of problem we solve.

THE ICE IS ONLY HALF THE JOB

GETTING IT AROUND A BUSY SITE IS THE OTHER HALF.

Gates. Passes. Security. Several bars all asking for ice at once. Large event sites are rarely as simple as “turn up here”, so we plan the useful bits before the vehicles leave.

We’ll probably ask more questions than you expected. Where can we unload? Who has the pass? Which bar needs what? Slightly annoying now, much better than a lost driver when the gates are about to open.

Too much communication? Definitely.

We can live with that. We’d rather you know exactly what’s happening than leave you wondering.

LOTS OF ICE?Pallet loads and more than one vehicle when the numbers call for it.
MORE THAN ONE BAR?We can plan different drops across the same site.
AWKWARD GATE?Send the passes, timings and exact drop point.
OPEN ALL WEEKEND?We can build planned top-ups around the event.
DON’T JUST TAKE OUR WORD FOR IT

WE’VE BEEN THROUGH THE GATES.

Silverstone. Goodwood Racecourse. Goodwood Motor Circuit. Oxted Festival. Smoked & Uncut. Blue Reef Festival. Loudout. Events at Canterbury Cathedral. That isn’t every job — we’d be here all day — but it gives you the idea.

TELL US WHAT YOU KNOW

WE’LL WORK OUT WHAT THE ICE NEEDS TO DO.

Exact numbers? Lovely. Rough idea? Also fine. Give us the date, site, expected crowd, bars or traders and whatever storage you have, and we’ll help turn it into a delivery plan.

Where and when?The site, delivery window and whether you need one drop or several.

What’s the ice doing?Drinks, bottle chilling, traders, displays — cubed, crushed or both.

Where can we actually unload?The right gate, one useful contact and any pass or security information.
