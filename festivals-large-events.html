THE RIGHT ICE FOR EACH OPERATION

CUBED, CRUSHED
OR BOTH.

Cubed ice is the usual choice for drinks service and bottle chilling. Crushed ice suits speciality cocktails, food displays and operations that specifically need a finer bed of ice.
